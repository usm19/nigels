## IDEA 1 — Subcontractor-side payment control

**Payapps (Autodesk)** is the gravity well. UK subcontractor plans run from pay-per-claim to "Ultimate" unlimited tiers (Pay as you go / Basic / Plus / Standard / Premium / Ultimate), priced by number of contracts, first self-managed claim free monthly — but exact GBP figures are deliberately gated (https://www.payapps.com/uk/pricing-subcontractors/). Critically, Payapps is main-contractor-led: Capterra reviews complain "the software should be more subcontractor friendly, with subcontractors having to pay the subscription," "cost is too expensive for subcontractors," and admins report awkward conversations charging subbies (https://www.capterra.com/p/238884/Payapps/reviews/). Subbies experience it as a portal imposed on them, not a tool that works for them — that resentment is your wedge.

**BuildQS** (https://app.buildqs.com/) is the closest direct threat: subcontractor-side AFP automation, retention ledger with release schedules (6+6, 12-month, custom) plus automatic release reminders, cash-flow forecasting, Xero draft invoices with retention invoices auto-created, 30-day trial. Pricing is not public, no visible reviews, and the "20+ years helping construction SMEs" framing suggests an accountancy spin-out — early stage, minimal traction signals. **Site Samurai** (https://www.sitesamurai.co.uk/pricing) is a new broad UK SME platform at £99/month Starter with unlimited users, AFP including Construction Act s.111 Pay Less Notice tracking, CIS/HMRC verification and insurance monitoring — broad but shallow, growth strategy is aggressive SEO comparison pages. **LazyQS** (https://www.lazyqs.co.uk/) is adjacent only: AI tender/contract review flagging payment-term risk, not live payment ops. **C-Link** (https://c-link.com/) is main-contractor procurement. **Planyard** (from ~$20-35/user/mo, retention tracking + CIS, Xero-first — https://planyard.com/pricing) is contractor cost-control, not notice-deadline defence. **Price A Job** is domestic estimating; **EasyBuild** and **Access Coins** are implementation-heavy enterprise ERPs with opaque pricing (https://www.itqlick.com/easybuild/pricing) — out of the 5-80-staff price band.

**Retention chasing + payer monitoring:** nobody credible combines them. **Retention Track** (https://retentiontrack.com/) does the ledger but is AU/NZ-built with UK "developing legislation" support; retention *recovery* is a services market (Hay's Collections https://www.hayscollections.com/services/retention-recovery/). Payer-risk monitoring is sold as separate credit intelligence (Top Service https://www.top-service.co.uk/) — with 3,912 construction insolvencies in 12 months and 277 in January 2026 alone (https://bricks-bytes.com/corporate/uk-construction-insolvency-crisis-2025/), no SME SaaS bundles Companies House/insolvency alerts on payers with the retention ledger.

- **Unserved slice:** an automated retention ledger + chasing engine + payer insolvency monitoring for subbies — the combination exists nowhere as SaaS.
- **Scariest competitor:** BuildQS, because it is already subcontractor-side with a retention ledger and Xero integration and just needs the chasing/monitoring layer.
- **Moat 12 months out:** thin on features but real via Construction Act/notice-deadline domain depth and accountant-channel distribution; expect BuildQS/Site Samurai to converge, so speed matters.
- **Whitespace score: 7/10.**

## IDEA 2 — AI quoting co-pilot for precision engineering / fabrication

**CloudNC** (London, $92.4M raised from Autodesk, Lockheed Martin, British Patient Capital — https://www.cloudnc.com/news-room/cloundnc-raises-45million) is the scary one: CAM Assist in 1,000+ shops by Sept 2025, and **Quote Agent** is a free-trial standalone web app doing DFM review, machining-risk flags and draft quotes from CAD/PDF (https://www.cloudnc.com/quote-agent). But it is CNC-machining-centric with bespoke, unpublished pricing — it does not do laser nesting, bend/weld/finish routing or sheet-metal cost build-up. **Paperless Parts** has no meaningful UK footprint, custom non-per-seat pricing favouring multi-year commitments (https://www.paperlessparts.com/pricing/); Practical Machinist threads show polarised sentiment — long-term users cite quoting ROI, sceptics balk at cost and sales approach (https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/what-is-the-deal-with-paperless-parts.431259/). **SecturaFab** is US-centric, no free trial, custom/perpetual pricing (https://www.capterra.com/p/209793/SecturaFAB/). The sheet-metal slice is *not* empty though: **Lantek iQuoting** is a cloud DXF-driven quoting SaaS backed by Lantek Expert nesting with UK operations (https://www.lantek.com/uk/blog/lantek-iquoting-sheet-metal-fabrication), and **Tempus Tools ToolBox** does laser/tube/3D-unfold quoting plus customer self-service portals, marketed in the UK via AILU (https://tempustools.com/products/toolbox/, https://ailu.org.uk/elegant-laser-cutting-quoting-solution-from-tempus-tools/). **Statii** (Chesterfield) is the beloved cheap UK job-shop ERP whose quoting is manual build-up, not geometry/AI-driven (https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/www.statii.co.uk). **Tangle** is an AI-native job-shop ERP with 40+ manufacturers and a UK branch registration (https://www.tangle.io/, https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/BR023506).

- **Unserved slice:** quote-from-inbox (email + PDF drawing) for mixed fabrication work — laser *plus* bending, welding, assemblies and powder-coat — priced monthly for 5-30-person UK shops, which Lantek/Tempus (laser-only) and CloudNC (machining-only) all skirt.
- **Scariest competitor:** CloudNC — UK-based, Autodesk-backed, using free Quote Agent as a wedge into exactly this buyer.
- **Moat 12 months out:** weak; quoting accuracy compounds slowly and incumbents have geometry engines you cannot rebuild solo, so you would survive only in the messy multi-process niche they ignore.
- **Whitespace score: 5/10.**

## IDEA 3 — Temp-desk operations layer for small agencies

The stack today is fragmented by price tier. **ETZ** (timesheets→invoicing→payroll, Xero/QuickBooks, undisclosed pricing, "cut back-office costs 85%" — https://getetz.com/) and **RSM InTime** (400+ agency clients, £3bn+ billed, bespoke pricing — https://www.rsmuk.com/campaign/intime) own mid-market pay-and-bill. **Ubeya** starts ~$50/mo but is shift/event-staffing-centric (https://www.ubeya.com/pricing-ubeya). CRM temp modules are tier-gated: **Firefish** £80-105/user/mo with add-on modules from £150 (https://www.firefishsoftware.com/pricing); **Vincere** £69-349/user/mo with Pay & Bill in upper configurations (https://esremedia.co.uk/blog/bullhorn-alternatives-uk-recruiters). A long tail of affordable UK point tools covers pieces: Timesheet Portal (custom pricing, https://www.timesheetportal.com/recruitment/prices/), TempAlly, RecGenie, Dataflo, and **PrimePRO** which embeds TrustID RTW checks with expiry reminders (https://primepro.net/); **RTWcomply** does RTW registers for £9-15/mo flat (https://rtwcomply.co.uk/). **TempaGoGo and uTrack: no meaningful verifiable footprint — non-factors.** **SafeRec** is the key scoper: real-time forensic payslip/RTI auditing and umbrella certification with a *free* agency due-diligence report (https://saferec.co.uk/) — it does NOT do timesheets, RTW or registration packs; it owns only the umbrella-audit layer. JSL is now live (in force 6 April 2026, agencies jointly liable for umbrella PAYE — https://www.contractoruk.com/news/jsl-umbrella-rules-now-in-force; REC pushing real-time provable verification — https://www.rec.uk.com/our-view/news/news-our-business-partners/umbrella-company-compliance-what-joint-and-several-liability-means-recruitment-agencies-and-how-prepare).

- **Unserved slice:** one affordable (~£150-300/mo flat) system stitching timesheet chasing + RTW/visa expiry + registration packs + a JSL evidence file for 5-30-seat agencies who currently juggle three tools and spreadsheets.
- **Scariest competitor:** SafeRec — free to agencies, already the JSL brand, and one product decision away from expanding into agency ops.
- **Moat 12 months out:** moderate — compliance workflow depth and audit-trail lock-in stick well, but every CRM vendor is bolting on "JSL-ready" messaging this year, so the window is roughly now to mid-2027.
- **Whitespace score: 6/10.**

## IDEA 4 — Supplier-side AI order-entry for food wholesalers

This space is contested by four funded players, all active in the UK. **Rekki Connect** (London) already converts email, voicemail, PDF and portal orders with claimed 90% processing-time reduction (bakery Seven Seeded saves 25 hrs/week — https://rekki.com/suppliers/connect, https://www.hospitalityandcateringnews.com/2024/03/ai-solution-will-revolutionise-order-processing-for-food-industry-suppliers/). **Choco** ($211M raised, Berlin) has retreated from the restaurant side and pivoted entirely to distributor-side AI — OrderAgent/Autopilot pushing orders straight into ERPs, an OpenAI showcase customer, 21,000+ distributors incl. UK (https://choco.com/uk/stories/suppliers/choco-ai, https://openai.com/index/choco/). **Orderlion** (Vienna, FWD member — https://www.fwd.co.uk/members/service-providers/orderlion) does WhatsApp/SMS/email/voicemail AI Inbox for 500+ suppliers, published pricing ~$788-2,146/month (https://www.cbinsights.com/company/orderlion). **Fresho** (AU + UK/Ireland) ships OrderPilot AI order entry with setup fee + volume-based subscription (https://www.fresho.com/gb/supplier/ai-order-entry). **Growyze** is buyer-side stock control, not relevant; **Merlin (Aptean)**, KCPOS and Erudus are the incumbent UK wholesale ERP/EDI/data layer, i.e. integration targets (https://www.aptean.com/en-GB/solutions/erp/products/merlin-business-software). The honest answer to "does an affordable tool exist": *almost* — Orderlion's ~£600+/month floor and Choco/Rekki's mid-market focus leave the 10-50-person wholesaler on Sage 50 or Xero underserved, and **Sage 50 sales-order integration is the unavoidable build** (demand proven by OrderEase — https://www.orderease.com/integrations/sage-50); none of the AI players lead with Sage 50.

- **Unserved slice:** sub-£300/month WhatsApp-and-voicemail order capture writing sales orders natively into Sage 50 for small UK wholesalers.
- **Scariest competitor:** Choco — massive capital, OpenAI partnership, and its entire company now bet on exactly this product.
- **Moat 12 months out:** near-zero — order-extraction accuracy is commoditising fast and any incumbent can drop prices into your niche; only Sage 50 integration grit plus local Birmingham distribution defends you, briefly.
- **Whitespace score: 3/10.**

## Whitespace ranking

**Idea 1 (7/10)** — clearest unserved combination (retention chasing + payer monitoring), weak early rivals, painful macro tailwind. **Idea 3 (6/10)** — real regulatory forcing function, but race against SafeRec and CRM vendors. **Idea 2 (5/10)** — viable only in the multi-process fab niche, under a well-funded UK shadow. **Idea 4 (3/10)** — four funded UK-active competitors; avoid unless you go hyper-local Sage 50-native.

# Round 3 — The 85+ Hunt: Three Sweeps and Four Skeptic Verdicts

> Compiled 31 July 2026. Goal: find a product scoring ≥85/100 on the strict rubric
> (retention ×3.0, cold-email ×2.0, pain ×1.5, whitespace ×1.5, build+low-touch ×1.5,
> Birmingham ×0.5) with near-irrelevant weaknesses. Method: three parallel sweep agents
> generated candidates with sub-scores; every candidate claiming ≥84 was then handed to
> an independent adversarial verifier instructed to refute it. Sources cited inline are
> as reported and should be re-verified before external use.

## Headline result

**No candidate survived verification at 85+.** Every sweep-claimed score collapsed
under adversarial checking, in the same three ways: a bundled incumbent hiding inside
cheaper software, a free "good-enough" substitute, or a regulatory/API maintenance
treadmill that breaks the founder's hands-off requirement.

## Sweep summaries (what was searched)

**Sweep 1 — office-based compliance niches** (accountancy AML, law-firm SRA, FCA
Consumer Duty, apprenticeship providers, architects CPD). Best claims: SRA suite 86,
Consumer Duty vault 84, apprenticeship funding-evidence tracker 81. Rejected in-sweep:
accountancy AML (~78, AMLCC £346/yr + Firmcheck 50p/client + crowded field), ECCTA IDV
(one-and-done, free GOV.UK One Login), architects CPD (free ARB/RIBA tools), MTD
(platform-owned).

**Sweep 2 — manufacturing-adjacent deterministic products** (cert traceability, ISO/AS
audit evidence, FAIR/PPAP generation, RFQ triage, supplier portals). Best claims:
CertVault ~85; RFQ triage/quote-chaser 79 (whitespace 6.5 — feature of every job-shop
ERP; revisit only as a module); ISO/AS audit pack 78 (isoTracker ~$10/user, GageList
free tier). Rejected: FAIR generation (Net-Inspect prime-mandated, 1factory $75/user;
drawing-ballooning OCR = accuracy treadmill), calibration-only (GageList free/79),
supplier-portal helpers (closed portals, ToS treadmill).

**Sweep 3 — wildcards via complaint mining** (nurseries, dental, block management,
apprenticeships, MOT garages, charities, ESG, sponsor-licence compliance, children's
homes, Martyn's Law). Best claims: fire-safety block-agent log 84, DEFRA waste tracking
84, sponsor-licence vault 82 (whitespace 4.5 — Borderless, Complygate, SponsorPro et
al.). Rejected: nurseries (Famly £79/Blossom £49 saturation), dental (Agilio iComply,
2,600 practices), MOT garages (MOT Juice £299.90/yr incumbent), apprenticeship MIS
(OneFile cheap + entrenched), children's homes Reg 44 (Clearcare £150/mo, 3,000 homes),
charity Gift Aid (free HMRC process, bundled in CRMs), SME carbon (crowded, episodic),
Martyn's Law (procedures, not recurring records).

## Full skeptic verdicts (key evidence)

### SRA suite 86 → 62
- CONFIRMED: SRA firm register API is free, daily-updated, includes business emails
  (attribution-only terms). Pain confirmed (86.6% of inspected firms not fully
  compliant; 151 enforcement outcomes; £565k+ fines 2025, 70% citing FWRAs).
  Willingness-to-pay confirmed — consultant retainers ~£1,000/mo; £150–250/mo software
  plausible.
- REFUTED: whitespace. Riliance (Access Group) 1,500+ firms; Teal Tracker (2-yr
  minimum), Osprey compliance module, LexisNexis Practice Compliance, Retibo. Compliance
  Caddy is £150+VAT/mo positioned as the economy option — the claimed £79–149 gap
  undercuts the floor rather than filling a void.
- ADJUSTED: PECR — sole practitioners/traditional partnerships are "individual
  subscribers" (consent needed), shrinking the mailable pool; legal cold email datapoint:
  3 deals from 1,300 emails. SRA's own free FWRA/RA templates are the true low-end
  competitor. FCA is taking over SRA AML supervision — permanent content maintenance.

### CertVault ~85 → 42.5
- REFUTED: whitespace (MetalTrace serves fabricators too at $5k/$500-mo; GoSmarter
  MillCert Reader £295/mo UK; Statii/QuickWorks/Fitfactory/PSL Datatrack bundle CofC
  packs at ≤£116/mo). Demand (Practical Machinist owners use folders/ERP — nobody buys
  standalone cert tools; the buying event is the MRP purchase).
- CONFIRMED AS LETHAL: free folder trees satisfy ISO/EN traceability; features that beat
  folders (auto heat-number extraction, missing-cert flags) need OCR + job BOM data —
  MRP territory and an accuracy treadmill.

### Fire-safety log 84 → 54
- REFUTED: whitespace (PlanRadar "Fire Safety Platform of the Year 2024"; SafetyCulture
  free fire-door templates ~£20/user; FireArrest ~£29.75 + free tier; IgnisTrack
  £35–49; RiskBase from £12.50/assessment; Bolster; Ringley built its own).
- ADJUSTED: Reg 10 checks only apply >11m buildings; Responsible Person is usually the
  freeholder/RMC, not the agent; ~870 managing-agent firms nationally (TPI ~350) →
  £50k needs 3–4% national penetration. FireDoorReady waitlist targets the identical
  ICP with live SEO — a threat, not a bonus.

### Waste tracking 84 → 43
- CONFIRMED: dates statutory (receivers Oct 2026; carriers Oct 2027; regs laid May 2026;
  public beta live April 2026). Daily statutory recording is sticky once installed.
- REFUTED: whitespace (WasteBolt £24.99/mo no-contract self-serve for small receivers;
  ~25 DEFRA-listed providers, 7 PAT-passed by Mar 2026; PaperRoute £220/SkipRoute £199
  mid-market). Free DEFRA spreadsheet route persists to ~Oct 2027. EA register CSV has
  no emails; skip yards are phone-culture. Draft API + PAT + Phase-2 scope = treadmill
  through 2028. Willingness-to-pay anchored ~£25/mo (DEFRA's own charge: £26/yr).

## Method

15+ verticals researched across four rounds. Every candidate whose researched score
reached ~73+ was handed to an independent adversarial verifier instructed to refute its
load-bearing claims with primary evidence (pricing pages, registers, legislation,
forums). Six candidates received full skeptic passes.

## The structural finding

**A verified 85/100 does not exist under the current constraint set.** Six independent
verifications produced the same three kill patterns every time:

1. **The bundling trap** — any workflow worth paying for is already a feature inside a
   cheaper horizontal or vertical incumbent (Statii, Access, Timesheet Portal).
2. **The free-substitute trap** — regulators and platforms give away templates,
   spreadsheets, registers, and free tiers precisely where pain is regulatory
   (SRA templates, DEFRA spreadsheet, SafetyCulture, SafeRec, CloudNC).
3. **The hands-off contradiction** — compliance pain implies changing rules; changing
   rules imply permanent content maintenance. AI-differentiation implies accuracy
   support. Both violate "bugs and features only."

Markets that look empty are empty for a reason (tiny TAM, free substitute, or
treadmill); markets with proven willingness-to-pay have incumbents. High verified
scores are *earned through distribution position*, not found through category choice.

## Options on the table

- **Option A (recommended): build the verified leader.** SRA Compliance Register Suite,
  hyper-niched to 1–4-partner firms, self-serve at ~£149/mo, with the "your data always
  with you" auto-export as the trust centrepiece. Weaknesses are known and bounded:
  compete on reach (the SRA's free daily firm register with emails; monthly SRA fine
  announcements as email hooks) against an enterprise-focused incumbent; accept
  ~1–2 weeks/year of rule-tracking content updates as the only real breach of
  "hands-off". £50k = ~30–35 retained firms of 9,000+, verified as arithmetically easy
  and temporally realistic over 12–18 months.
- **Option B: relax the cold-email-only constraint** (add phone + local visits).
  Reopens West Midlands manufacturing/construction where density is unmatched — but the
  verified whitespace kills (Tempus/Statii bundling; Payapps/retention ban) remain.
- **Option C: keep hunting.** Not recommended: six-for-six verifications show the
  85-claim always deflates; further sweeps buy delay, not a better answer.

## Pre-build validation sprint (recommended before committing to Option A)

One week, before any code: 200-firm PECR-compliant test campaign off the SRA register
(Ltd/LLP only), a landing page with the £149 price visible, and 5 conversations with
COLPs at 1–4-partner firms. Kill criteria: <1% positive reply AND no pre-orders →
revisit Option B rather than build.

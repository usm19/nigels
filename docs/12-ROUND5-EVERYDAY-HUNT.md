## The structural law (now demonstrated 9 times out of 9)

Everyday businesses buy ONE core system (booking, dispatch, till, rota, PMR, payroll) —
and every compliance or admin need either gets bundled into that system at zero marginal
price, is satisfied by paper/free regulator templates, or recurs too rarely (annual
audits, licence renewals) to sustain a monthly subscription. High-frequency + high-pain
+ unserved + stable rules + email-bookable buyers is a combination that, in this
project's verified evidence base, exists only where money itself is the workflow —
which is why the construction payment engine is the sole ~75 survivor.

## Definition note for the founder's decision

The excluded term was "major construction." The verified leader does NOT sell to major
construction companies — its customers are 5–80-person everyday trade firms
(electricians, plumbers, groundworks, shopfitters) and the product defends them AGAINST
major contractors. Whether that falls inside or outside the founder's exclusion is a
founder call, not a research question.
